## 10. Phase 6 — Validate the Actual P04 Workflow

The intended business process is:

1. Obtain invoice information from an authorized source.
2. Retrieve the relevant Purchase Order.
3. Retrieve the associated Purchase Receipt.
4. Retrieve the Purchase Invoice.
5. Compare ordered, received and invoiced quantities and prices.
6. Identify missing records, quantity discrepancies, price discrepancies, tax/freight differences and other supported exceptions.
7. Produce an evidence-based explanation and recommended resolution.
8. Hand off the findings for human review.

Validate only scenarios supported by available records and authorized test procedures.

### Clean three-way match

Verify that the PO, receipt and invoice are correctly linked and compare their relevant fields. Confirm whether the agent identifies a match accurately.

### Price variance

If a suitable authorized record exists, verify whether the agent detects a difference between the relevant PO and invoice prices and explains the calculation.

### Quantity variance

If a suitable authorized record exists, verify whether the agent detects differences between ordered, received and invoiced quantities.

### Missing or inconsistent documents

Verify whether the agent identifies unavailable documents, broken links, ambiguous values and incomplete evidence without inventing missing information.

### Evidence dossier

Verify whether the agent can report:
- Source document identifiers.
- Relevant line items.
- Observed quantities and prices.
- Calculation method.
- Actual discrepancy.
- Missing evidence.
- Uncertainty and confidence limitations.
- Recommended human review.

Do not invent company tolerance thresholds. Until an approved policy is provided, treat detected non-zero discrepancies as exceptions requiring human review.

Do not create artificial financial transactions in the live environment. If no suitable records exist, report the test as `BLOCKED` and request approval for a separately authorized test environment or controlled test-data plan.
